# Spooky Toy Cabinet

Concept angle: turn the supplied white-background lineup into a theatrical Halloween collector cabinet, balancing clean Amazon product clarity with playful orange-and-purple stage energy.

- Focal element: the real supplied duck lineup and exact crops from it.
- Edge anchors: compact chapter labels at upper left, a thin progress rule along the lower edge, and occasional oversized ghost type behind the product.
- Supporting detail: crescent arcs, tiny star sparks, registration dots, and short utility labels; no web-style cards or fake UI.
- Background treatment: plum-black solid field with localized orange or purple radial pools, never a full-screen linear gradient.
- Product panels: warm-white, deep soft shadow, 34px corners, and enough breathing room to preserve the original catalog photography.
- Type pairing: Archivo Black 400 for expressive statements; Space Mono 400/700 for labels and supporting copy.
- Motion character: fast controlled camera moves, short cascades, and one playful overshoot on character arrivals; ambient movement remains slow and finite.
- Avoid: neon cyberpunk, horror gore, generic pumpkin clip-art, invented product counts, fake glow claims, and any copied Amazon graphics.
